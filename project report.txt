PRICE WAR IN PUNE: A STATISTICAL COMPARISON OF KIRANA, DMART & BLINKIT

Course: B.Sc. Statistics – Practical Project 2026 Location: Pune,
Maharashtra

1. Abstract

This project compares the retail prices of 15 daily essential grocery
items across three popular purchasing channels in Pune: Local Kirana
Stores, DMart, and Blinkit. The objective is to determine whether a
significant difference exists in prices and identify the most economical
option for students. Descriptive statistics and One-Way ANOVA were used
for analysis.

2. Objectives

1.  To collect primary price data for commonly used grocery items.
2.  To calculate Mean, Median, and Standard Deviation.
3.  To perform a One-Way ANOVA test to determine whether price
    differences are statistically significant.
4.  To visualize the data using a Grouped Bar Chart and Boxplot.

3. Data Collection

Method: Primary data collected on 22 September 2026 from a local Kirana
Store near campus, DMart Aundh, and Blinkit (Pune location).

Sample Items: Milk, Bread, Eggs, Maggi, Coke, Lays, Rice, Dal, Oil,
Sugar, Tea, Biscuit, Curd, Paneer, Water.

Total Observations: 15 × 3 = 45 price observations.

4. Descriptive Statistics

  Channel   Mean Price (₹)   Standard Deviation   Median Price (₹)
  --------- ---------------- -------------------- ------------------
  Kirana    63.26            38.25                56
  DMart     59.06            37.15                54
  Blinkit   70.40            39.56                61

Key Findings: - DMart was the cheapest option for all 15 items. -
Blinkit was the most expensive option for all 15 items. - Average saving
in DMart compared to Kirana: ₹4.2 per item (6.6%). - Average Blinkit
premium compared to Kirana: ₹7.1 per item (11.2%).

5. Inferential Statistics – One-Way ANOVA

Null Hypothesis (H₀): Mean prices of Kirana, DMart, and Blinkit are
equal. Alternative Hypothesis (H₁): At least one channel has a different
mean price.

Results: - F-Statistic = 0.32 - P-Value = 0.7304

Interpretation: Since p-value > 0.05, the null hypothesis cannot be
rejected. The difference in average prices is not statistically
significant at the 5% level. However, DMart remains practically cheaper
across all sampled items.

6. Data Visualization

Figure 1: Grouped Bar Chart comparing prices across all items. Figure 2:
Boxplot showing price distribution across channels.

7. Conclusion

DMart is the most economical option among the three channels. Although
ANOVA does not show a statistically significant difference, practical
savings are meaningful. A student spending approximately ₹3000 per month
on groceries could save around ₹330 monthly by choosing DMart over
Blinkit.

8. Limitations

1.  Small sample size (15 items).
2.  Prices may vary across locations.
3.  Promotional discounts were not included.
4.  Data was collected on a single date.
